5.1.2 Published LCA studies using previous versions of the method

Multiple studies have been published which use the physical effects on biota CFs. However, it is important to note that some of these studies were conducted using previous versions of the CFs, and therefore, do not reflect the latest update (i.e., the PEBBLE method). These studies cover the following sectors:

  • packaging (Corella-Puertas et al. 2022, 2023; Erradhouani et al. 2026; Galafton et al. 2025),
  • fishing (Askham et al. 2026),
  • agriculture (Louvet et al. 2026),
  • textile (Fois et al. 2026; Pellengahr, Corella-Puertas, et al. 2025; Pellengahr, Ghannadzadeh, et al. 2025; Saadi et al. 2025; Saadi and Boulay 2026),
  • sports (Zeilerbauer et al. 2024),
  • household items (Hamann et al. 2026),
  • and wastewater treatment (Alibekov et al. 2025; Puhar et al. 2025)
